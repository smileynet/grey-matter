Wait, I don't understand where you've got to here. Re-pitch that: **add the context I was missing, don't just reword the same message** (the gap is usually a premise or step you assumed, not the phrasing). Say it in short, active-voice sentences, one meaning per word (the spirit of Simplified Technical English), and use the ubiquitous language from `GLOSSARY.md` so it stays precise rather than vaguely simpler. If the repo has more than one glossary, follow its glossary map to the right one.
