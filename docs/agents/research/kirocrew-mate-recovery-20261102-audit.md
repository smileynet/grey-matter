## Findings and limits

- The native SID, persisted parent and new Mate turn are corroborated by distinct native session histories, slot snapshots, the crew-log projection, and Mate messages. This is strong evidence for this graceful restart case.
- The fabricated-task failure and outside-worktree write are corroborated by native tool history and the actual fixture bytes. This makes the conclusion against unattended task fidelity well founded.
- The corrected commit's two-file scope is directly verified. Test execution is supported by the root's reported independent check, not by this auditor's rerun; a worker's own “tests pass” prose should not be treated as independent acceptance.
- The receipt is sanitized and source companion is explanatory; raw evidence is private and sensitive. This audit did not copy it or expose gateway tokens/log URLs.
- Limits remain: one host/configuration/model, graceful between-turn restart, a bounded startup window, no crash or in-flight recovery, no durable pending-report test, and no matched conductor comparison. The reference 0.12.0 checkout is not evidence for installed 0.11.1.

No unsupported claim was found in the bounded Ticket 156 conclusion. Preserve its distinction between successful recovery and failed autonomous planning, plus its stated unknowns.
