## Revision history and design assessment

The initial audit blocked on four points: generic usage-limit wording; no explicit incomplete terminal outcome; conversation-derived monitor history presented without a durable source distinction; and overstatement of what the invocation checker proves. The frozen revision resolves all four directly. The remaining conversation-sourced history is explicitly labeled non-reproducible and its current-state source is bounded to the dashboard record.

No disproportionate gate or critic-preflight recommendation was found. The proposal favors small on-demand deterministic checks, explicitly avoids expanding pre-push gates, and asks critics to write acceptance expectations before reading the candidate so review binds to exact evidence. It also says a small document review need not create a nested review tree. These controls are proportionate to the stated candidate-binding and independence failures.

The proposed design coherently evolves the shipped skill, keeps one conductor and tkt as ledger, binds review to exact candidate/evidence, counts reviewers against finite slots, and separates work, verification, cleanup and delivery. User-and-agent invocation remains appropriate only with the proposal's explicit existing-authorization invariant and separate composition/selection scenarios. No duplicate ticket, claim, role or project-control defect was found in the sampled material.

Coverage is sampled, not a full audit of the orchestration stream: I re-read the core skill, invocation conventions/checker, implement and implement-spec frontmatter, key cited Kiro/model research, proposal 17, current dashboard passages and the exact revision points. I did not inspect every ticket/evidence receipt, exercise native goals, test skill selection or inspect private raw coordination logs. Official docs are rolling; OpenAI and Claude goal/skill pages were fetched on the audit date. No tests, settings, tickets, model probes, installs, commits or pushes were performed.
